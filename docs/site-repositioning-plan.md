# Site repositioning plan

## Goal

Reposition pozdnyakov.io around Fractional CIO / external IT leadership while keeping
AI Audit as a distinct entry product and preserving its useful interactive experience.

## Implementation

1. Centralize verified profile facts, contacts, pricing visibility, navigation, CTA labels,
   service descriptions, cases, and bilingual content.
2. Add a shared advisory shell, route-specific SEO, typed analytics events, a qualified
   contact form, and reusable service/case/90-day sections.
3. Implement Russian and English routes for home, Fractional CIO, AI Audit, cases,
   IT diagnostic, about, and contact.
4. Move the existing AI Audit experience to `/ai-audit`, retain its interactions, correct
   ambiguous claims and calculator defaults, and add a Fractional CIO cross-link.
5. Add server-side contact validation, spam protection, and basic rate limiting while
   preserving the existing CRM adapter and email fallback.
6. Update sitemap, manifest, structured data, internal links, 404 behavior, and
   print-friendly First 90 Days resources.
7. Validate typecheck, focused lint, production build, route responses, internal links,
   keyboard/form behavior, and representative mobile/desktop layouts.

## Safe defaults requiring owner approval

- New Fractional CIO and Interim CIO price ranges are configured but hidden.
- AI Audit keeps its already public price range.
- Availability messaging is disabled until a current value is confirmed.
- The existing `ai@pozdnyakov.io` mailbox remains the public contact email.
- No calendar URL is shown until one is configured.
- Former employer names are used only as career history; no employer logos are added.
- The ambiguous count of approximately 40 AI projects is replaced with qualitative wording.
